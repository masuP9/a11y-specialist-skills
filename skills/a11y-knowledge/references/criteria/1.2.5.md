# 1.2.5 Audio Description (Prerecorded)

- Scope: Prerecorded video in synchronized media
- Materials: Video/main audio, described audio/versions, static descriptions, usable pauses, and player behavior
- Automated check: Tracks and alternate-version links are candidates. Play and compare main audio, description, and video
- Sources checked: 2026-11-02. WCAG: Recommendation of 2024-12-12; Understanding / Techniques: living published pages. The following are summaries, not quotations.

## Evidence

### 1.2.5-E01 — Check the AA audio route and synchronized-media scope

- **Type / source:** [Normative — Audio Description (Prerecorded)](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#audio-description-prerecorded), [Normative — synchronized media](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#dfn-synchronized-media), [Understanding — Intent](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/audio-description-prerecorded.html#intent).
- **Summary:** Convey important visual information missing from the main audio through description. No addition is necessary when the main audio already suffices. Clearly labeled media alternatives to text are excluded from synchronized media's definition.
- **Conditions / limits:** Do not ignore that definition merely because this SC lacks 1.2.3's explicit exception clause. Nearby text alone does not establish exclusion. Providing full text for an ordinary video under 1.2.3 does not transfer conformance to this criterion.

### 1.2.5-E02 — Accept audio description beyond separate tracks

- **Type / source:** [Technique — G78 “Description / Tests”](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/general/G78#description) (Sufficient), [G173 “Description / Tests”](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/general/G173#description) (Sufficient using a more specific technique), [G226 “Description”](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/general/G226#description) (Sufficient with G173).
- **Summary:** Options include selectable/default described audio, another video version, and narration integrated during production. Convey needed actions, on-screen text, and outcomes without obscuring important original audio.
- **Conditions / limits:** Check actual access and playback of alternate versions. Do not require an additional track element or a particular button.

### 1.2.5-E03 — Check G203 for a static single-speaker video

- **Type / source:** [Technique — G203 “Description / Tests”](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/general/G203#description) (Sufficient for 1.2.3 and 1.2.5).
- **Summary:** With no important time-based visual information, such as a single speaker against an unchanged background, programmatically associated static text may convey unheard speaker identification, context, opening/closing credits, etc.
- **Conditions / limits:** Do not use this when multiple speakers alternate and only visuals identify them. A video containing important slides/demonstrations does not qualify merely because someone is talking. `aria-describedby` is one association method, not the only one.

### 1.2.5-E04 — Distinguish usable pauses from extended description

- **Type / source:** [Failure — F113 “Description / Tests”](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/failures/F113#description), [Normative — Extended Audio Description (Prerecorded)](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#extended-audio-description-prerecorded).
- **Summary:** Check F113 when important visual information is missing and suitable pauses remain. Do not assume pauses are usable when too short, distant from the relevant scene, or needed for important sounds. Extended descriptions that pause video are addressed by AAA criterion 1.2.7.
- **Conditions / limits:** F113's “less than 2 seconds” is illustrative, not a fixed threshold. Absence of this failure alone does not prove whole-SC conformance. Report gaps addressable in ordinary pauses separately from information requiring extended description.

### 1.2.5-E05 — Separate description-track presence from audible delivery

- **Type / source:** [Technique — H96 “Description”](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/html/H96#description) (Advisory).
- **Summary:** A descriptions track is timed text for speech output. Check that it is actually delivered non-visually and that users can hear the needed description.
- **Conditions / limits:** H96's September 2023 native-support note is not a current test of every browser. Play it in the target player/supporting implementation and check missing or truncated speech. An attribute check alone does not establish SC conformance.

## Judgment lines

- Pass ordinary cases where the main audio conveys all important visual information. Do not flag merely missing separate description. Described audio/versions conveying needed information also pass. [Evidence: 1.2.5-E01–E02]
- Clearly labeled media alternatives to text that add no information are out of scope. Check correspondence to the source text and labeling. [Evidence: 1.2.5-E01]
- Pass with associated static description when G203's conditions hold. Do not apply this route when only visuals convey changing speakers or steps. [Evidence: 1.2.5-E03]
- Fail when main audio, description, and G203 all fail to convey important visual information and usable pauses remain. If pauses and their use are unknown, obtain that scene's audio/video for review. [Evidence: 1.2.5-E01–E04]
- Where usable pauses for standard description have been checked and exhausted, and remaining information requires pausing the video for extended description, do not fail AA for that remaining gap alone. Record the scope checked for AA and remaining information, and separate AAA criterion 1.2.7. “Lots of dialogue” alone does not establish this treatment. [Our interpretation: reporting policy separating AAA extension requirements from AA. Evidence: 1.2.5-E04]
- A track in the DOM is not delivered description if users cannot hear it in the target environment and no other route works. If playback is unverified, leave for review. [Evidence: 1.2.5-E02, E05]

## Judgment examples

- **Pass:** The main audio names each target, action, and displayed result as operations occur. No additional track is needed. [Evidence: 1.2.5-E01–E02]
- **Pass:** A single-speaker greeting against an unchanged background has associated text with unheard name, role, and credits. There is no important time-based visual information. [Evidence: 1.2.5-E03]
- **Out of scope:** A video clearly identified as an alternative to explanatory text conveys only that text's information. [Evidence: 1.2.5-E01]
- **Fail:** Important steps appear only on screen; suitable pauses exist but the audio does not describe them. Full text meeting 1.2.3 does not substitute for this criterion. [Evidence: 1.2.5-E01, E04]
- **Do not flag that gap at AA:** Usable pauses contain descriptions, but remaining details need a pause in playback for explanation. Record the remaining information and need for extension, and separate AAA assessment. [Our interpretation: example of the reporting policy above. Evidence: 1.2.5-E04]
- **Needs review:** Only a descriptions track is found; speech output is unverified. Operate the player and compare audible content with the video. [Our interpretation: inspection example. Evidence: 1.2.5-E05]

## When building

- Integrate important visual information into narration during production. When adding description, avoid obscuring original audio and provide a route to select/play it. [Evidence: 1.2.5-E02]
- Apply G203 only after checking applicability; verify audible delivery of track-based implementations in the target environment. [Evidence: 1.2.5-E03, E05]

## When checking

- **Confirmed:** Results after comparing main audio, audio/static descriptions, and applicability. Record usable pauses when applying F113. [Evidence: 1.2.5-E01–E05]
- **Remaining:** Visual-only information, usable pauses, access/playback of other versions, truncated synthesized speech.
- **Do not overclaim:** Neither missing nor present tracks alone settle conformance. Record timestamps, missing information, and pause conditions; distinguish the 1.2.3 text route and AAA criterion 1.2.7. [Evidence: 1.2.5-E01–E05. Evidence format is our policy]
