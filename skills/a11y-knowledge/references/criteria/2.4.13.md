# 2.4.13 Focus Appearance

- Scope: Keyboard focus indicators when evaluating AAA
- Needed: Unfocused component/subcomponent geometry, colors at identical positions before/after focus, qualifying area, UA modifications
- Automated checks: Current focus-indicator-check style/image differences cannot decide this criterion

- Sources checked: 2026-11-01. WCAG: 2024-12-12 Recommendation; Understanding / Techniques: live WCAG 2.2 pages. Summaries, not quotations.

## Rationale

### 2.4.13-E01 — Measure qualifying area, not just declared thickness

- **Type / source:** [Normative — WCAG 2.2, Focus Appearance](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#focus-appearance), [Understanding — Minimum area](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/focus-appearance.html#minimum-area).
- **Summary:** Evaluate an area satisfying both the unfocused component/subcomponent’s 2 CSS px perimeter equivalent and 3:1 change at identical positions. Outlines are not the only shape.
- **Conditions / limits:** Deeply inset 2px lines can lack area. Two sides or `outline-width: 2px` alone do not establish a pass. Exclude external shadows/glows from component geometry.

### 2.4.13-E02 — Adjacent contrast alone is insufficient

- **Type / source:** [Understanding — Relationship with Non-text Contrast](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/focus-appearance.html#relationship-with-non-text-contrast).
- **Summary:** 1.4.11 compares neighbors; 2.4.13 compares identical positions before/after focus. Either can pass while the other fails.
- **Conditions / limits:** Record compared states/positions and qualifying area. An image-difference count is not that area.

### 2.4.13-E03 — Check backgrounds for UA exceptions

- **Type / source:** [Normative — Exceptions](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#focus-appearance), [Understanding — Exceptions](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/focus-appearance.html#exceptions).
- **Summary:** Distinguish UA indicators authors cannot adjust from indicators whose appearance and background color authors have not modified.
- **Conditions / limits:** An unchanged outline alone does not establish the latter exception. Check background changes. Do not assume identical exceptions to 1.4.11.

### 2.4.13-E04 — Two-color indicators also have conditions

- **Type / source:** [Technique — C40, Description / Tests](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/css/C40#tests) (Sufficient).
- **Summary:** C40 checks 9:1 between colors, each band at least 2 CSS px, and placement outside the component over one solid background. Either band can independently supply area and contrast.
- **Conditions / limits:** Black and white alone do not establish a pass. Images, gradients, or overlapping components need individual measurement. Check potential loss of box-shadow-only indicators in forced colors.

## Judgment lines

- Check exceptions, then both area and change contrast where applicable. Our full-perimeter/two-side recommendation is not a substitute for these AAA conditions. [Evidence: 2.4.13-E01–E04]
- In AA audits, report as additional improvement opportunities, not AA failures. Evaluate 2.4.7 and 1.4.11 separately. [Our interpretation: reporting scope; supporting evidence: 2.4.13-E02]

## When building

- Retain [2.4.7’s recommendation](./2.4.7.md) for an identifiable indicator on the component itself. For AAA, leave margin in the area meeting change contrast. [Our interpretation: implementation policy; supporting evidence: 2.4.13-E01]
- When using C40, check background conditions and both bands; preserve the roles of outline and box-shadow. [2.4.13-E04]

## When checking

- **Conclusive:** Verified insufficient qualifying area or change contrast, with exceptions ruled out. [2.4.13-E01, 2.4.13-E03]
- **Remaining:** Component/subcomponent geometry, complex backgrounds, and scope of UA modifications. Unmeasurable cases need review. [2.4.13-E01–E04]
- **Do not overclaim:** Neither `visualDiff` nor detected style changes verify area or 3:1. [Our interpretation: tool mapping; implementation: [axe-format.ts](../../../../packages/a11y-audit/src/utils/axe-format.ts)]
