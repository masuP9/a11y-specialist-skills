# 1.2.2 Captions (Prerecorded)

- Components in scope: `<video>` (no ARIA role. Videos in embedded iframes are handled separately)
- Inputs needed: `<track>` kind, `muted` / `controls` attributes, video content (whether captions are burned in, whether they match the audio)
- Automated checks: axe `video-caption` (reports no violations)

## Rationale

- [Understanding 1.2.2](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/captions-prerecorded.html) ([Japanese translation](https://waic.jp/translations/WCAG22/Understanding/captions-prerecorded.html))
  - The scope is prerecorded "synchronized media" (audio and video synchronized). Video-only without audio (the range of 1.2.1) and media provided as an alternative for text are out of scope.
  - Captions include not only dialogue but also sound information needed to understand the content, such as sound effects.
  - Captions burned into the video (open captions) also satisfy the requirement.
- [G87: closed captions](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/general/G87), [G93: open captions](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/general/G93), [H95: track element](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/html/H95)

## Judgment lines

- What is judged is "whether a prerecorded video with audio has captions that convey the audio content". [Understanding]
- Even with a `<track kind="captions">`, do not mark conformant on that alone. Whether the caption content matches the audio cannot be known without watching the video. [Our interpretation]
- Even without a `<track kind="captions">`, do not mark non-conformant on that alone. Captions may be burned into the video. [Understanding] (open captions satisfy the requirement)
- `kind="subtitles"` is translated subtitles and `kind="descriptions"` is audio description text; neither substitutes for captions. [Understanding]
- A video that can be understood without hearing the audio (`muted` with no `controls`, only playing automatically as a background or decoration) is out of scope. [Understanding] (without audio it is not synchronized media)
- In conclusion, do not mark conformant or non-conformant from the tree and attributes alone; mark only needs review (confirm the caption content / confirm there are no burned-in captions) or not applicable. [Our interpretation]

## When building

- Add `<track kind="captions" srclang="ja" label="日本語">` to videos with audio, and write sound effects too.
- When burning in captions, also providing a transcript of the same content helps with 1.2.1 and search.
- Make decorative background videos `muted` and do not include audio.
- For embeds such as YouTube, confirm the provider's captions exist. Auto-generated captions are often not accurate enough.

## When checking

- **Settled**: none. axe `video-caption` returns incomplete when there is no captions track and pass when there is, but neither is used as a verdict.
- **Remains**: whether the caption content matches the audio, and whether there are burned-in captions. Play the video to confirm.
- **Do not assert**: do not mark conformant or non-conformant by the presence or absence of a track alone. Downgrade any such verdict to needs review.
