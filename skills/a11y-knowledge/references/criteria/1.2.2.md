# 1.2.2 Captions (Prerecorded)

- Scope: Prerecorded synchronized media with audio, including embedded players
- Materials: Video and audio, caption content/display/synchronization, and its relationship to source text
- Automated check: axe-core 4.14.0 `video-caption`. Separate track detection from the criterion verdict
- Sources checked: 2026-11-02. WCAG: Recommendation of 2024-12-12; Understanding / Techniques: living published pages. The following are summaries, not quotations.

## Evidence

### 1.2.2-E01 — Verify the exception for media alternatives to text

- **Type / source:** [Normative — Captions (Prerecorded)](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#captions-prerecorded), [Understanding — Intent](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/captions-prerecorded.html#intent).
- **Summary:** The exception requires media to be an alternative to text and clearly identified as such. Check that it adds no information beyond the source text.
- **Conditions / limits:** This does not mean an alternative to video. A nearby transcript alone does not establish this relationship. Assess audio-only/video-only under 1.2.1 and live media under 1.2.4 separately.

### 1.2.2-E02 — Check audio information and synchronization, not just presentation mode

- **Type / source:** [Technique — G87 “Description / Tests”](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/general/G87#description) (Sufficient using a more specific technique), [G93 “Description / Tests”](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/general/G93#description) (Sufficient), [Failure — F8 “Description”](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/failures/F8#description).
- **Summary:** Both closed and open captions qualify. Convey dialogue and necessary speaker/sound information in sync with the video. F8 addresses missing dialogue or important sounds, while allowing readable simplification that preserves meaning.
- **Conditions / limits:** Presence alone is insufficient. A standalone transcript does not replace synchronized captions. Do not universally require verbatim wording or every ambient sound.

### 1.2.2-E03 — Do not decide caption adequacy from track kind alone

- **Type / source:** [Technique — H95 “Description”](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/html/H95#description) (Sufficient with G87).
- **Summary:** `captions` is appropriate, but `subtitles` can meet this criterion when it conveys the necessary sound information in the language of the audio.
- **Conditions / limits:** Dialogue-only subtitles are insufficient when important sounds are missing. Do not confuse audio-description text in `descriptions` with captions for auditory content. Failing H95's test alone does not establish SC failure.

## Judgment lines

- Do not universally require additional captions for audio provided as an equivalent alternative to silent video. See [1.2.1-E01](./1.2.1.md) for this limited case; distinguish it from ordinary audiovisual video. [Evidence: 1.2.1-E01]

- Normally assess prerecorded video with audio by checking caption display, content, and synchronization. Video without audio is outside this criterion. `muted` or missing `controls` does not prove absence of audio; inspect the audio track and actual presentation. [Evidence: 1.2.2-E01. Checking beyond attributes is our inspection policy]
- Do not flag media clearly identified as an alternative to text when it adds no information. Merely transcribing a video afterward does not establish the exception. [Evidence: 1.2.2-E01]
- Accept open captions and `subtitles` conveying the required auditory information. Neither presence nor absence of `captions` alone settles conformance. [Evidence: 1.2.2-E02–E03]
- Do not decide by automatic versus manual production. Check actual meaning-changing transcription errors, missing dialogue/important sounds, and synchronization defects. Adequate content and synchronization pass; confirmed absence or loss of required information in applicable media fails. If playback is unverified, record the missing evidence for review. [Evidence: 1.2.2-E01–E03. Production-independent inspection is our policy]

## Judgment examples

- **Pass:** Open captions convey dialogue and necessary speaker/sound information in sync with video. No track is needed. [Evidence: 1.2.2-E02]
- **Pass:** `kind="subtitles"` conveys dialogue and important sounds in the language of the audio. Separate markup recommendations from the SC verdict. [Evidence: 1.2.2-E03]
- **Exception; do not flag:** A video clearly labeled as an alternative to explanatory text conveys only that text's information. [Evidence: 1.2.2-E01]
- **Fail:** A training video that is not an alternative to text captions dialogue but omits an alarm that cues the next action. No other adequate captions are available. [Evidence: 1.2.2-E02]
- **Needs review:** A captions track exists, but its file could not be retrieved and display is unverified. Play the video to check loading, content, and synchronization. [Our interpretation: inspection example. Evidence: 1.2.2-E02–E03]

## When building

- For closed captions, use `track kind="captions"` or another implementation with necessary sounds, speaker information, and synchronization. Open captions also qualify. [Evidence: 1.2.2-E02–E03]
- Enable and check captions in embedded videos too. Remove audio from background video when it is unnecessary. A transcript can provide additional access but does not replace synchronized captions. [Evidence: 1.2.2-E01–E02. Implementation recommendations are ours]

## When checking

- **What automation settles:** In axe-core 4.14.0, `video-caption` returns pass when the target video contains a track with `kind="captions"`, otherwise incomplete. It does not verify caption file loading, quality, or synchronization and cannot settle SC conformance. Implementation checked: `captionEvaluate` and the rule's `none: ['caption']`.
- **Remaining:** Audio presence, the text-alternative relationship and labeling, player operation, and audio/caption comparison. Record video/caption references, playback timestamps, and specific omissions/displayed content. One still image cannot settle an entire video. [Evidence: 1.2.2-E01–E03. Evidence format is our policy]
- **Do not overclaim:** Do not extend axe passes to the whole SC or turn incomplete into Fail. Once manual checks establish the conditions, do not keep the verdict undecidable. [Evidence: 1.2.2-E01–E03]
