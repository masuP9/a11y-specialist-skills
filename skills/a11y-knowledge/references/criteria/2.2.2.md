# 2.2.2 Pause, Stop, Hide

- Scope: whole page
- Inputs needed: measurement (auto-play-detection from `@a11y-skills/audit`)
- Automated checks: auto-play-detection

## Rationale

- [Understanding 2.2.2](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/pause-stop-hide.html) ([Japanese translation](https://waic.jp/translations/WCAG22/Understanding/pause-stop-hide.html))
  - "Moving, blinking, or scrolling" content that starts automatically, lasts more than 5 seconds, and is presented in parallel with other content needs a means to pause, stop, or hide it.
  - Auto-updating content needs a means to pause, stop, or hide it, or to control the frequency of the update.
  - The exception is when the movement is essential (a loading indicator and so on).
  - Sound is the range of 1.4.2.
- [G4: can be paused and resumed](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/general/G4), [G11: a means to stop](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/general/G11), [F16: scrolling that cannot be stopped](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/failures/F16)

## Judgment lines

- What is judged is "whether content that starts automatically and moves for more than 5 seconds has a means to pause, stop, or hide it". [Understanding]
- Measurement only goes as far as listing candidates for moving content from screen diffs. Whether a means exists is decided by looking at the content (needs review). [Our interpretation]
- Videos, carousels, animated GIFs, and news tickers tend to become candidates. Loading indicators are an exception. [Understanding]

## When building

- Give automatically moving content (carousels, background videos, animations) a visible "Pause" button. Stopping on hover alone is not enough.
- Support `prefers-reduced-motion` and do not animate for users who have set reduced motion.
- Do not animate things that do not need to move.

## When checking

- **Settled**: none.
- **Remains**: for each auto-play-detection candidate, check whether it moves for more than 5 seconds and whether there is a means to stop it.
- **Do not assert**: do not mark conformant without measurement. Do not treat sound problems as 2.2.2 (1.4.2).
