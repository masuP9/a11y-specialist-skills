# 2.2.2 Pause, Stop, Hide

- Scope: Automatically starting movement, blinking, scrolling, and updating presented alongside other content
- Needed: Duration, concurrent content, and results of stopping or other controls
- Automated check: auto-play-detection (screen differences, candidate stop controls, and behavior verification)

- Sources checked: 2026-11-01. WCAG uses the 2024-12-12 Recommendation; Understanding / Techniques use the living WCAG 2.2 pages. The following are summaries, not quotations.

## Evidence

### 2.2.2-E01 — Do not apply the movement five-second condition to automatic updates

- **Type / source:** [Normative — WCAG 2.2 “Pause, Stop, Hide”](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#pause-stop-hide), [Understanding — “Intent”](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/pause-stop-hide.html#intent).
- **Summary:** Movement, blinking, and scrolling have a duration condition of over five seconds; automatic updating has no five-second condition. Check automatic starting and presentation alongside other content for both. Controlling update frequency is also an option for automatic updates.
- **Conditions / limits:** Indirect triggers such as hover, focus, and scrolling content into view also count as automatic starting. Do not conclude there is nothing in scope from an initial-load check alone.

### 2.2.2-E02 — Check that the page remains usable while paused

- **Type / source:** [Understanding — explanation of pause in “Intent”](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/pause-stop-hide.html#intent), [Technique — G4 “Description” and “Tests”](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/general/G4.html#tests) (Sufficient).
- **Summary:** Pausing only while focused and resuming when the user operates other content is insufficient. Check that other content remains usable while the pause persists.
- **Conditions / limits:** Buttons are not the only method; documented keyboard commands can work. Real-time information can resume at its current state, without saving and replaying all paused updates. Evaluate hover-only pausing for this usability and input access as well.

### 2.2.2-E03 — Do not automatically exempt loading indicators as essential

- **Type / source:** [Normative — WCAG 2.2 “Pause, Stop, Hide,” Note 4](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#pause-stop-hide), [Understanding — “Intent”](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/pause-stop-hide.html#intent).
- **Summary:** A loading animation can be essential during preloading when no users can interact and the lack of a progress indicator would suggest a frozen or broken page.
- **Conditions / limits:** Do not automatically exempt loading indicators when other page content remains usable. Even blinking lasting no more than five seconds needs separate assessment under flashing criteria such as 2.3.1.

## Judgment lines

- Separate movement from automatic updates and assess scope conditions, controls, and the essential exception. Check audio separately under [1.4.2](./1.4.2.md). [Evidence: 2.2.2-E01–E03]
- The tool identifies candidates from about six seconds of screen differences, actually clicks identified stop controls, and briefly verifies stopping. Retain the existing policy of reviewing candidates; do not extend a no-detection pass to slow updates, small changes, or other states. [Our interpretation: Tool mapping. ## When building

- Prefer visible pause buttons and avoid hover-only pausing. This implementation recommendation does not exclude other effective stopping/hiding methods. [Our interpretation: Author implementation policy. Supporting evidence: 2.2.2-E02]
- Avoid unnecessary motion and support prefers-reduced-motion. Supporting that preference alone does not establish a stop mechanism for all users. [Our interpretation: Author recommendation. Supporting evidence: 2.2.2-E01]

## When checking

- **Confirmed:** Results of matching start conditions, duration, concurrent content, controls, and exceptions. [2.2.2-E01–E03]
- **Remaining:** Update-frequency control, persistent pause after focus moves, and availability of other content during loading.
- **Do not assert:** Do not exclude automatic updates for lasting no more than five seconds. Screen differences alone do not establish non-conformance.
