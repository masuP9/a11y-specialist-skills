# 1.4.4 Resize Text

- Scope: whole page
- Inputs needed: measurement (zoom-200-check from `@a11y-skills/audit`)
- Automated checks: zoom-200-check

## Rationale

- [Understanding 1.4.4](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/resize-text.html) ([Japanese translation](https://waic.jp/translations/WCAG22/Understanding/resize-text.html))
  - Content and functionality must not be lost when enlarged up to 200%. It may be satisfied by browser zoom.
  - Examples of loss: text clipped, overlapping, or hidden; interactive components no longer pressable. Horizontal scrolling appearing is not itself asked under 1.4.4 (the range of 1.4.10).
- [G142: supporting browser zoom](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/general/G142), [F69: clipping or overlapping when enlarged](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/failures/F69), [F80: only the text does not enlarge](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/failures/F80)

## Judgment lines

- If there is a place where text is clipped, overlaps, or is hidden at 200%, non-conformant. [Understanding] (F69)
- If horizontal scrolling merely appears, do not mark non-conformant under 1.4.4 (check under 1.4.10). [Understanding]
- All measurement candidates are needs review. Whether a place that looks clipped really loses content is decided visually. [Our interpretation]

## When building

- Do not fix font size in `px`; write it in relative units (`rem`).
- Do not put text in a box with a fixed height. Do not hide text with `overflow: hidden`.
- Do not write `maximum-scale=1` or `user-scalable=no` (do not block pinch zoom).

## When checking

- **Settled**: none.
- **Remains**: decide visually per candidate whether there is clipping, overlapping, or hiding at 200%.
- **Do not assert**: do not mark conformant without measurement. Do not mark horizontal scrolling non-conformant under 1.4.4.
