# 1.4.13 Content on Hover or Focus

- Scope: Additional content shown/hidden in response to receiving/removing hover or focus
- Materials: Trigger and additional content, overlap, dismissal operation, pointer route, persistence conditions
- Automated check: `content-on-hover-check`. Compare Escape, timed waits, and center-to-center movement with actual conditions
- Sources checked: 2026-11-02. WCAG: Recommendation of 2024-12-12; Understanding / Techniques: living published pages. The following are summaries, not quotations.

## Evidence

### 1.4.13-E01 — Distinguish additional content from exclusions

- **Type / source:** [Normative — Content on Hover or Focus](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#content-on-hover-or-focus), [Understanding — Intent / Additional Notes](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/content-on-hover-or-focus.html#intent).
- **Summary:** Assess custom tooltips and similar content; presentation controlled by the UA and unmodified by the author is excepted. A skip link itself appearing on focus is not additional content.
- **Conditions / limits:** Distinguish native title tooltips from author-created popups using the same string. Click-only content is outside this criterion. Do not reassign a focus-taking modal to this SC; check [3.2.1](./3.2.1.md) and other applicable criteria.

### 1.4.13-E02 — Do not extend dismissal exceptions to other conditions

- **Type / source:** [Normative — Dismissible](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#content-on-hover-or-focus), [Understanding — Dismissible](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/content-on-hover-or-focus.html#dismissible).
- **Summary:** Check dismissal without moving pointer hover or keyboard focus. Input-error content and content that neither obscures nor replaces other content are exceptions to this dismissal condition.
- **Conditions / limits:** Check obstruction of meaningful content in the trigger too. Ordinary content over only whitespace/pure decoration does not require dismissal. These exceptions do not waive hoverability or persistence.

### 1.4.13-E03 — Check access to additional content and continued display

- **Type / source:** [Technique — SCR39 “Tests”](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/client-side-script/SCR39#tests) (Sufficient), [Failure — F95 “Description / Tests”](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/failures/F95#description), [Normative — Hoverable / Persistent](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#content-on-hover-or-focus).
- **Summary:** Hover-triggered content must allow moving the pointer onto it. Display continues until the trigger is removed, the user dismisses it, or information becomes invalid. Dismissal may use a documented key other than Escape or activation of the trigger.
- **Conditions / limits:** Do not mechanically require hoverability for focus-only content. A fixed duration such as three seconds is not sufficient. A now-invalid “busy” message may disappear when processing completes.

## Judgment lines

- Pass when all applicable conditions hold. Fail a confirmed unmet condition without an applicable exception. [Evidence: 1.4.13-E01–E03]
- Missing Escape alone is not Fail; check other dismissal without movement and dismissal exceptions. A close button requiring focus movement alone does not demonstrate this condition. [Evidence: 1.4.13-E02–E03]
- Check disappearance en route to hover content or replacement by another data point's popup preventing reading. If only the tool's straight-line route failed, try an actual usable route. [Evidence: 1.4.13-E03. Route rechecking is our inspection policy]
- Fail content disappearing solely on a timer while still valid and hovered/focused. Lasting beyond the test wait does not establish conformance; check disappearance conditions. [Evidence: 1.4.13-E03]

## Judgment examples

- **Pass:** A hover description can itself be hovered and stays visible; Escape dismisses without moving focus/hover. [Evidence: 1.4.13-E02–E03]
- **Do not flag missing dismissal:** A description occupies only whitespace and neither obscures nor replaces other content. Hoverability and persistence hold. [Evidence: 1.4.13-E02–E03]
- **Out of scope:** The skip link itself appears on focus. Assess any separate additional explanation independently. [Evidence: 1.4.13-E01]
- **Fail:** A hover-triggered input error disappears when the pointer moves onto it. Do not extend its dismissal exception to hoverability. [Evidence: 1.4.13-E02–E03]
- **Fail:** A still-valid description disappears after five seconds while still hovered. A three-second test pass does not override this. [Evidence: 1.4.13-E03]
- **Needs review:** Only a failed Escape test is available. Obtain overlap, error content, and other dismissal operations. [Our interpretation: inspection example. Evidence: 1.4.13-E02–E03]

## When building

- Arrange content and display conditions to permit pointer movement onto it; provide dismissal without movement when required. Do not hide still-valid information solely after a timer. [Evidence: 1.4.13-E02–E03]
- Check keyboard operation of hover functionality separately under [2.1.1](./2.1.1.md). [Evidence: 1.4.13-E01]

## When checking

- **Implementation checked:** `runContentOnHoverCheck.ts`, `normalizeContentOnHoverCheck`, and `rule-registry.ts`. Failed conditions become `incomplete`. Dismissal tests only Escape, persistence only `persistMs` (default three seconds), and hoverability a straight line from trigger center to content center. Dismissal that also moves focus remains a candidate.
- **Confirmed:** Manual results after comparing condition-specific exceptions and actual behavior. [Evidence: 1.4.13-E01–E03]
- **Remaining:** Delegated listeners, cross-origin CSS, iframe/shadow roots, updates inside existing elements or opacity-based displays, `untested` / `aborted`, and longer timers. `title` / `aria-describedby` / `aria-haspopup` alone do not produce candidates.
- **Do not overclaim:** Tool inapplicable does not establish SC exclusion; a three-second pass does not establish persistence. Record applicability, exceptions, and untested states. [Evidence: 1.4.13-E01–E03]
