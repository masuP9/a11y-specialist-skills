# 1.4.11 Non-text Contrast

- Scope: Visual information needed to identify components/states or understand graphics
- Needed: Required parts, adjacent colors, display states, author modifications and exceptions
- Automated checks: non-text-contrast-check (boundary/icon candidates; not comprehensive state testing)

- Sources checked: 2026-11-01. WCAG: 2024-12-12 Recommendation; Understanding / Techniques: live WCAG 2.2 pages. Summaries, not quotations.

## Rationale

### 1.4.11-E01 — Not every border needs measurement

- **Type / source:** [Normative — WCAG 2.2, Non-text Contrast](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#non-text-contrast), [Understanding — User Interface Components / Boundaries](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/non-text-contrast.html#user-interface-components).
- **Summary:** Evaluate information needed to identify components or states. A hit-area boundary is unnecessary when text or other content identifies the control.
- **Conditions / limits:** A faint border alone is insufficient to fail. Assess required focus/selection indicators separately. Verify inactivity or unmodified UA appearance before applying exceptions.

### 1.4.11-E02 — Compare adjacent colors, not states

- **Type / source:** [Understanding — Relationship with Focus Visible](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/non-text-contrast.html#related-focus).
- **Summary:** Compare the focused indicator with adjacent colors, not its previous color.
- **Conditions / limits:** Inside, outside, and boundary indicators have different neighbors. Do not always compare with the page background. Assess state changes/area under [2.4.13](./2.4.13.md), visibility under [2.4.7](./2.4.7.md).

### 1.4.11-E03 — Identify required graphic parts and their neighbors

- **Type / source:** [Technique — G207, Description / Tests](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/general/G207#tests), [G209, Description / Tests](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/general/G209#tests) (Sufficient for making graphical content understandable).
- **Summary:** Measure icons against their backgrounds and meaningful adjoining graphical regions. G207 distinguishes recognizable icons with low-contrast parts removed; G209 also uses separating borders.
- **Conditions / limits:** One color below 3:1 does not automatically fail the whole graphic. Check whether remaining parts convey the necessary meaning. Distinguish essential presentation from decoration. [Normative exception](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#non-text-contrast)

### 1.4.11-E04 — Do not round up to pass

- **Type / source:** [Understanding — Notes in Intent](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/non-text-contrast.html#intent).
- **Summary:** Do not round ratios up to 3:1. Use UA/style colors rather than antialiased edge pixels alone.
- **Conditions / limits:** Uncertain composited/adjacent colors need review. Distinguish numeric conformance from improving very thin lines.

## Judgment lines

- Verify required information, adjacent colors, and inapplicable exceptions before failing below 3:1. Unnecessary borders/decorations alone do not establish failure. [Evidence: 1.4.11-E01, 1.4.11-E03, 1.4.11-E04]
- Do not pass or fail 1.4.11 solely on differences between states. [Evidence: 1.4.11-E02]
- `low` / `no-boundary` / `undetermined` are candidates. Review unmeasured `otherVisuals` and states. Zero candidates do not establish a criterion-wide pass. ## When building

- Identify which cues convey components/states, then check their adjacent colors. [1.4.11-E01, 1.4.11-E02]
- Separating borders can distinguish required regions of multicolored graphics. [1.4.11-E03]

## When checking

- **Conclusive:** Low contrast with the target and color pair verified. [1.4.11-E01–E04]
- **Remaining:** Focus/selection/error states, charts, image backgrounds, pseudo-elements, internal check marks, iframes, and Shadow DOM. The current check does not cover these comprehensively. [Our interpretation: tool scope]
- **Do not overclaim:** Distinguish component boundaries from state indicators. Reaching 3:1 does not establish conformance with 2.4.7 or 2.4.13. [1.4.11-E01, 1.4.11-E02]
