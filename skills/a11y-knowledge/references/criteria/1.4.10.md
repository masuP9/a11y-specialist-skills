# 1.4.10 Reflow

- Scope: whole page
- Inputs needed: measurement (reflow-check from `@a11y-skills/audit`)
- Automated checks: reflow-check

## Rationale

- [Understanding 1.4.10](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/reflow.html) ([Japanese translation](https://waic.jp/translations/WCAG22/Understanding/reflow.html))
  - At a width of 320 CSS px (height of 256 px for vertical text), content and functionality must be usable without scrolling in two directions. Equivalent to 400% zoom.
  - The exception is "content that requires two-dimensional layout for usage or meaning": data tables, maps, diagrams, games, presentations, toolbars, and so on. Only the part that falls under the exception may scroll horizontally.
  - Avoiding two-directional scrolling by hiding content is not allowed.
- [C32: rearranging with media queries](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/css/C32), [F102: content or functionality lost](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/failures/F102)

## Judgment lines

- A place that needs horizontal scrolling at 320px width is decided by whether it falls under an exception (table, map, diagram, toolbar, and so on). If not, non-conformant. [Understanding]
- Even if the place that needs horizontal scrolling is an exception, if it makes the area outside it (body text, navigation) scroll horizontally too, non-conformant. [Understanding]
- All measurement candidates are needs review. Whether it falls under an exception is decided by looking at the content. [Our interpretation]

## When building

- Do not fix widths in `px`. Wrap with `max-width: 100%` and `flex-wrap` / `grid`.
- Wrap tables in a horizontally scrolling box (only the table is the exception). Do not drag the body text along.
- Give fixed-width images and embeds (iframes) `max-width: 100%`.
- Check on a real 320px-wide device or at 400% browser zoom.

## When checking

- **Settled**: none.
- **Remains**: for each candidate, check whether the cause of horizontal scrolling falls under an exception and whether it drags the body text along.
- **Do not assert**: do not mark conformant without measurement. Do not mark a table's horizontal scrolling non-conformant.
