# 2.2.1 Timing Adjustable

- Scope: Time limits set by content, including server-side deadlines
- Needed: Deadline specifications and actual warning, turn-off, adjustment, and extension behavior
- Automated checks: time-limit-detector and axe meta-refresh (candidates)

- Sources checked: 2026-11-01. WCAG uses the 2024-12-12 Recommendation; Understanding / Techniques use the living WCAG 2.2 pages. The following are summaries, not quotations.

## Evidence

### 2.2.1-E01 — Distinguish tenfold adjustment from ten extensions

- **Type / source:** [Normative — WCAG 2.2 “Timing Adjustable”](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#timing-adjustable), [Technique — G180 “Tests”](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/general/G180.html#tests) (Sufficient).
- **Summary:** Check for turning off the limit before encountering it, adjusting it beforehand over a range at least ten times the default, or a warning with at least 20 seconds for a simple action to extend it at least ten times.
- **Conditions / limits:** Do not reinterpret extension as one tenfold extension. Check the warning period and repeated-extension conditions, not just the presence of an extension button.

### 2.2.1-E02 — Check essential, real-time, and over-20-hour exceptions individually

- **Type / source:** [Normative — WCAG 2.2 “Timing Adjustable”](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#timing-adjustable), [Understanding — “Intent” and “Notes regarding time limits”](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/timing-adjustable.html#intent).
- **Summary:** For real-time events, check whether an alternative to the limit is impossible; for essential limits, whether extension would invalidate the activity. The 20-hour exception means more than 20 hours, not exactly 20.
- **Conditions / limits:** Do not exempt something merely because it is called an exam or security measure. Ordinary session deadlines are in scope. Time-based authentication tokens can be essential; assess each function.

### 2.2.1-E03 — Check for an untimed alternative to disappearing content

- **Type / source:** [Understanding — temporary notifications and repeating content in “Intent”](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/timing-adjustable.html#intent).
- **Summary:** When the same information and functionality remain accessible without a time limit, disappearance alone does not require timing adjustment. A notification whose new message remains in an inbox is an example.
- **Conditions / limits:** Repetition alone does not suffice for a carousel; check access to and control of its content. Assess distracting automatic updates separately under [2.2.2](./2.2.2.md).

### 2.2.1-E04 — Do not use 20 seconds as a meta refresh conformance boundary

- **Type / source:** [Failure — F40 “Description” and “Tests”](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/failures/F40.html#tests), [F41 “Description” and “Tests”](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/failures/F41#tests).
- **Summary:** For delayed redirects and reloads, check actual behavior, turn-off / adjustment / extension mechanisms, and exceptions. Distinguish an immediate zero-second redirect from a time-limit failure.
- **Conditions / limits:** Twenty seconds is the time allowed to perform an extension action, not a safe meta refresh delay. A short Failure test does not replace checking normative exceptions and available controls.

## Judgment lines

- Match the deadline, alternatives, exceptions, and available turn-off / adjustment / extension conditions against the evidence. [Evidence: 2.2.1-E01–E04]
- time-limit-detector identifies timer, text, and meta-tag candidates. A timer alone does not establish a time limit. Do not extend a no-detection pass to server-side deadlines or unexamined states. [Our interpretation: Tool mapping. Implementation: [runTimeLimitDetector.ts](../../../../packages/a11y-audit/src/playwright/runTimeLimitDetector.ts)]
- Treat axe meta-refresh violations as requiring control and exception checks, not automatic non-conformance. In the inspected axe-core 4.14.0, `metaRefreshEvaluate` checks seconds, not page extension mechanisms. Do not use the former “confirmed within 20 seconds” rule. [Our interpretation: Tool mapping. Supporting evidence: 2.2.1-E04. Implementation inspected: `node_modules/axe-core/axe.js`]

## When building

- Avoid automatic meta refresh redirects/reloads and prefer user-controlled updating. [Our interpretation: Author implementation policy. Supporting evidence: 2.2.1-E04]
- Prefer turning off or adjusting deadlines beforehand; an extension approach needs at least 20 seconds after warning and at least ten extensions. [Evidence: 2.2.1-E01]

## When checking

- **Confirmed:** Results after checking the deadline, controls, alternatives, and exceptions; automatic candidates alone do not confirm a result. [2.2.1-E01–E04]
- **Remaining:** Warning period, repeated extension, server deadlines, and access to the same information/actions as a notification.
- **Do not assert:** Do not judge from a timer or an “exam” label alone. Do not mark conformant without observing behavior. [Our interpretation: Inspection policy]
