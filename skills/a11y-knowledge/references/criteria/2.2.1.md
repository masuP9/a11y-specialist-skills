# 2.2.1 Timing Adjustable

- Scope: whole page
- Inputs needed: measurement (time-limit-detector from `@a11y-skills/audit`)
- Automated checks: time-limit-detector, axe `meta-refresh`

## Rationale

- [Understanding 2.2.1](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/timing-adjustable.html) ([Japanese translation](https://waic.jp/translations/WCAG22/Understanding/timing-adjustable.html))
  - If there is a time limit, one of these must be possible: turn off, adjust (to at least 10 times the default), or extend (warned at least 20 seconds before, and extendable by at least 10 times with a simple action).
  - Exceptions are real-time events (auctions), essential time limits (exams), and limits longer than 20 hours.
  - Automatic refresh (meta refresh), session timeouts, and automatically advancing carousel transitions can also count as time limits.
- [G133: a checkbox to extend](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/general/G133), [G180: the limit can be adjusted](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/general/G180), [F40 / F41: meta refresh](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/failures/F41)

## Judgment lines

- Candidates for time limits (meta refresh, timers, session warnings) are decided by whether there is a means to turn off, adjust, or extend. [Understanding]
- Measurement only goes as far as listing candidates. Whether a means exists is decided by looking at the content (needs review). [Our interpretation]
- A meta refresh that automatically navigates within 20 seconds may be settled as non-conformant (axe `meta-refresh`). [Understanding] (F40)

## When building

- Do not auto-navigate or auto-refresh with `<meta http-equiv="refresh">`.
- For session expiry, show a warning before it expires (20 seconds or more before) and allow extending with a single action.
- Give automatically advancing content (carousels, news tickers) a means to stop or slow down (also check 2.2.2).

## When checking

- **Settled**: violations of axe `meta-refresh` are non-conformant.
- **Remains**: for each time-limit-detector candidate, check whether there is a means to turn off, adjust, or extend.
- **Do not assert**: do not mark conformant without measurement.
