# 1.4.6 Contrast (Enhanced) (AAA)

- Scope: When evaluating AAA, the visual presentation of text and images of text, including placeholder text and text shown on hover or focus
- Materials: The same as [1.4.3](./1.4.3.md) (text and background colors, font size and weight, background images, gradients and overlaps, the presentation in each state, themes, and whether an exception applies)
- Automated check: axe-core 4.14.0 `color-contrast-enhanced` (`wcag2aaa` / `wcag146`). It is not in audit's default WCAG tags (A/AA) and does not run from the CLI. Passes of `color-contrast` (1.4.3) do not keep the ratio, so text below 7:1 cannot be picked out from those results
- Sources checked: 2026-11-11. WCAG: Recommendation of 2024-12-12. Understanding updated 2026-10-04; G17 and G148 updated 2026-09-06; G174 updated 2026-06-28; G156 updated 2025-07-15; F24 updated 2026-10-04; F83 updated 2026-03-09 (living public pages). ACT rule 09o5cg is approved, updated 2023-08-30. The following are summaries, not quotations.

## Evidence

### 1.4.6-E01 — Only the thresholds differ from 1.4.3

- **Type / source:** [Normative — Contrast (Enhanced)](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#contrast-enhanced), [Normative — Contrast (Minimum)](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#contrast-minimum), [Term — large scale](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#dfn-large-scale).
- **Summary:** Text and images of text need 7:1, large text 4.5:1. The exceptions (part of an inactive component, pure decoration, not visible to anyone, part of a picture with significant other visual content, part of a logo or brand name) use the same wording as 1.4.3.
- **Conditions / limits:** The large-scale definition is shared with 1.4.3, and the CJK equivalent size is not defined.

### 1.4.6-E02 — Understanding also matches 1.4.3

- **Type / source:** [Understanding — Intent](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-enhanced.html#intent), [Understanding — Logos](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-enhanced.html#logos), [Understanding — Rationale for the Ratios Chosen](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-enhanced.html#rationale-for-the-ratios-chosen), [Understanding — Inactive User Interface Components](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-enhanced.html#inactive-controls).
- **Summary:** The explanations are the same as in the 1.4.3 Understanding: do not round the ratio, use colors from the user agent or styles, placeholder and hover/focus text are in scope, the logo exception and the treatment of logos made faint by author choice, and characters used as symbols are assessed under 1.4.11. 7:1 was chosen as the contrast needed by people with 20/80 visual acuity (the same reasoning by which people with 20/40 need 4.5:1).
- **Conditions / limits:** Even at 7:1, thin fonts can look fainter in practice because of anti-aliasing; a thicker font or a color pair with margin is encouraged (not required).

### 1.4.6-E03 — Ways to meet it and failures

- **Type / source:** [Technique — G17 "Description"](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/general/G17#description), [G17 "Tests"](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/general/G17#tests), [G148 "Description"](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/general/G148#description), [G174 "Description"](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/general/G174#description) (all Sufficient), [G156 "Description"](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/general/G156#description) (Advisory), [Failure — F24](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/failures/F24#description), [F83](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/failures/F83#description).
- **Summary:** G17 is 7:1 between text and background. When the background or letters vary in luminance, choose or shade the background around the letters so they keep 7:1 with the background behind them. Large text uses G18 (4.5:1). G148 (specifying neither text nor background color) and G174 (a switch to a sufficient-contrast presentation) are also ways to meet it. G156 (a technology whose user agents let users change colors) is Advisory and is not listed as a way to meet it on its own. F24 (specifying only one color) and F83 (text over background images) are Failures shared with 1.4.3.
- **Conditions / limits:** Techniques are not the only way. F24 and F83 follow the lines adopted in 1.4.3.

### 1.4.6-E04 — The scope of the ACT rule and axe

- **Type / source:** [ACT 09o5cg "Applicability"](https://www.w3.org/WAI/standards-guidelines/act/rules/09o5cg/#applicability), ["Assumptions"](https://www.w3.org/WAI/standards-guidelines/act/rules/09o5cg/#assumptions), ["Background"](https://www.w3.org/WAI/standards-guidelines/act/rules/09o5cg/#background).
- **Summary:** For each visible character, the rule checks whether the highest possible contrast between foreground and background is at least 7:1 (4.5:1 for large text). Text in disabled components is excluded. The assumptions and the meaning of a pass are the same as for 1.4.3's ACT afw4f7; passing the rule does not mean the criterion is satisfied.
- **Conditions / limits:** axe `color-contrast-enhanced` is mapped to this ACT rule, but do not assume an identical implementation. It does not run under audit's default tags (`DEFAULT_AXE_TAGS` in [constants.ts](../../../../packages/a11y-audit/src/constants.ts)).

## Judgment lines

- Text that is not exempt is an AAA failure if it is below 7:1 for normal text or 4.5:1 for large text. Compare without rounding. [Evidence: 1.4.6-E01, E02]
- Use 1.4.3's judgment lines as they are, reading only the thresholds as 7:1 / 4.5:1: the Japanese large-text line, how to read the inactive, decoration, and logo exceptions, measuring the letters likely to be lowest over background images and gradients, failing text-color-only or background-color-only specification (F24) only when an unreadable combination is reproduced with user color settings, the treatment of themes, `prefers-contrast`, and on-page switches, and not assessing 1:1 text. [1.4.3's judgment lines](./1.4.3.md#judgment-lines) are the source of truth; do not copy them here. [Author policy: the normative text and Understanding match 1.4.3 except for the thresholds, and F24 and F83 are shared. Each `[Author policy]` line in 1.4.3 carries over as is. Evidence: 1.4.6-E01–E03]
- Do not pass content only because users can change text and background colors in their browser. [Evidence: 1.4.6-E03. G156 is Advisory]
- Evaluate 1.4.6 only in audits whose target level includes AAA, or when asked to look at AAA. When you evaluate it, report it as AAA in a table separate from AA, and do not count it in AA Pass/Fail totals. In AA audits and reviews, do not list text between 4.5:1 and 7:1 even as improvement candidates. AA is judged under 1.4.3. [Author policy: reporting scope that keeps AAA results out of AA failures. Listing them in an AA audit needs an extra run or measurement and does not change the AA conclusion. This does not currently match 2.4.12 and 2.4.13, which list AAA results as additional improvement candidates in AA audits (to be handled in the cross-check). Evidence: 1.4.6-E01, E04]

## Judgment examples

- **Pass:** Body text is `#595959` on `#ffffff` (about 7.0:1). [Evidence: 1.4.6-E01]
- **AAA failure (passes 1.4.3):** Body text is `#767676` on `#ffffff` (about 4.54:1). It does not reach 7:1. Do not list it in an AA audit. [Evidence: 1.4.6-E01. Reporting scope is an author policy]
- **Pass:** A 24px normal-weight heading is `#767676` on `#ffffff` (about 4.54:1). It is large text, so 4.5:1 is enough. [Evidence: 1.4.6-E01]
- **AAA failure:** A 20px Japanese heading with `font-weight: 700` at 4.0:1. It is 14pt bold or more, so it is large text, but it does not reach 4.5:1. [Author policy: the Japanese and weight lines come from 1.4.3. Evidence: 1.4.6-E01]
- **Exempt, not reported:** Gray text in a `disabled` button, text on a street sign in a photo, the company name in the header logo image. [Evidence: 1.4.6-E01]
- **Improvement recommendation (not a failure):** Only `body { color: #222 }` is set, no background is specified anywhere, and no unreadable combination can be reproduced with user color settings. [Author policy: the F24 treatment comes from 1.4.3. Evidence: 1.4.6-E03]

## When building

- When aiming for AAA, give body text color pairs of at least 7:1. 1.4.3's "When building" applies as well (also specify a background color, use a band or outline for text over images, choose state and theme colors against the same threshold). [Evidence: 1.4.6-E01–E03]
- With thin fonts, avoid color pairs that only just reach 7:1; leave a margin. [Evidence: 1.4.6-E02]

## When checking

- **What automation settles:** When evaluating AAA, pass `tags` (for example `["wcag2aaa"]` or `["wcag146"]`) to the library's `runAxeAudit` to run `color-contrast-enhanced`. Read violations and incomplete results the same way as `color-contrast` in [1.4.3](./1.4.3.md#when-checking). Results from the CLI alone contain no material for 1.4.6 (running AAA rules from the CLI is tracked in [#187](https://github.com/masuP9/a11y-specialist-skills/issues/187)). [Evidence: 1.4.6-E04. Implementation: [runAxeAudit.ts](../../../../packages/a11y-audit/src/playwright/runAxeAudit.ts)]
- **When measuring:** The ratio depends only on the combination of text color, background color, and size category (normal or large), so measure each combination once. Do not measure every element with the same combination. Over images and gradients, measure the letters likely to be lowest, as in 1.4.3. [Author policy: inspection scope; the conclusion does not change within the same combination. Evidence: 1.4.6-E01]
- **Remaining:** Placeholders, hover and focus presentations, other themes, images of text, text over canvas or video, and iframe content. Treat them as in 1.4.3's "Remaining". [Evidence: 1.4.6-E02]
- **Do not assert:** Passing 1.4.3 does not mean 1.4.6 is met. Do not treat an axe pass or zero results as conformance with all of 1.4.6. [Evidence: 1.4.6-E01, E04]
