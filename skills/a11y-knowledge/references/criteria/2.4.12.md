# 2.4.12 Focus Not Obscured (Enhanced)

- Scope: Focused components and author-created coverings when evaluating AAA
- Needed: The whole component itself, coverings/translucent effects, actual presentation on focus
- Automated checks: focus-indicator-check (`focus-not-obscured-enhanced`)

- Sources checked: 2026-11-01. WCAG: 2024-12-12 Recommendation; Understanding / Techniques: live WCAG 2.2 pages. Summaries, not quotations.

## Rationale

### 2.4.12-E01 — Do not inherit the minimum criterion’s allowances

- **Type / source:** [Normative — WCAG 2.2, Focus Not Obscured (Enhanced)](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#focus-not-obscured-enhanced), [Understanding — Intent](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/focus-not-obscured-enhanced.html#intent).
- **Summary:** Partial obscuration by author-created content is assessed. This criterion does not include 2.4.11’s notes on user-opened/movable content.
- **Conditions / limits:** Partial visibility, dismissibility, or movability alone does not establish a pass. Do not report AAA failures as AA failures.

### 2.4.12-E02 — Inspect the component itself and translucent coverings

- **Type / source:** [Understanding — Translucent overlays and note in Intent](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/focus-not-obscured-enhanced.html#intent).
- **Summary:** External focus rings are not part of the component. Translucent, blurring, or dimming overlays can fail this enhanced criterion even when content remains recognizable.
- **Conditions / limits:** Readability alone does not rule out obscuration. Purely geometric overlap with complete transparency and no visual effect does not establish failure. Assess obscured external indicators separately under 2.4.7 and related criteria.

### 2.4.12-E03 — Verify full visibility after scrolling

- **Type / source:** [Technique — C43, Tests](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/css/C43#tests) (Sufficient).
- **Summary:** C43 tests minimum and enhanced separately; enhanced requires no hidden component portion.
- **Conditions / limits:** scroll-padding alone is insufficient. Check actual focus position and changing header/banner dimensions.

## Judgment lines

- Fail after verifying actual partial obscuration by author-created content. Do not reuse 2.4.11’s notes or the 99% threshold. [Evidence: 2.4.12-E01, 2.4.12-E02]
- `focus-not-obscured-enhanced` includes partial-obscuration candidates, but detection has minimum overlap areas and exclusions. Zero candidates do not establish a pass. [Our interpretation: tool mapping; implementation: [axe-format.ts](../../../../packages/a11y-audit/src/utils/axe-format.ts), [constants.ts](../../../../packages/a11y-audit/src/constants.ts)]

## When building

- Reserve space around fixed content and check full component visibility during forward/backward navigation. [Evidence: 2.4.12-E03; our interpretation: testing both directions]

## When checking

- **Conclusive:** Actual partial component obscuration, distinguished from obscuration of an external ring alone. [2.4.12-E01, 2.4.12-E02]
- **Remaining:** Translucent coverings, non-initial states, excluded targets, and overlaps below detection thresholds. [Our interpretation: scope management]
- **Do not overclaim:** Passing AA [2.4.11](./2.4.11.md) does not establish a pass for this AAA criterion. [2.4.12-E01]
