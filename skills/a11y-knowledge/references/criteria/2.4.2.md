# 2.4.2 Page Titled

- Scope: whole page
- Inputs needed: `<title>` (`details.title` from `page-snapshot`), headings (to read the topic), and a list of titles when there are multiple pages
- Automated checks: axe `document-title`

## Rationale

- [Understanding 2.4.2](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/page-titled.html) ([Japanese translation](https://waic.jp/translations/WCAG22/Understanding/page-titled.html))
  - The title must "describe the topic or purpose". That is the whole requirement.
  - The Understanding document considers a different title per page and the form "page name - site name" desirable, but they are not requirements.
  - The intent is that users can tell pages apart from a list of tabs or the history.
- [G88: descriptive titles](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/general/G88), [H25: title element](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/html/H25), [F25: a title that does not describe the topic](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/failures/F25)

## Judgment lines

- The axis of judgment is "does the title describe the page's topic or purpose". It does not require being different from other pages. [Understanding]
- Non-conformant if the title is only the site name, default text like "Untitled", a file name, or a URL, and the topic evident from the headings is not in the title. When the topic is the site itself, as on the top page, the site name alone is conformant. [Understanding] (F25)
- Non-conformant if the title describes a topic or purpose different from the topic evident from the headings (the heading is "Sorting and disposing of garbage" and the title is "Contact us"). When the topic is evident from the headings, do not mark needs review on the grounds that "it may describe the purpose". [Our interpretation] (from the pattern of misjudgments)
- The presence, order, separator, and length of the site name are not asked. [Understanding]
- Do not mark non-conformant solely because the title is the same as other pages. The same title continuing across pages with different content is a strong hint that it does not describe the topic, but pagination of search results and pages that serve the same content under different URLs may overlap. [Understanding]
- A missing or empty `<title>` is settled as non-conformant (axe `document-title`). [Understanding]

## When building

- Put the page's topic (the same as the h1 is fine) in `<title>`. The form "Page name | Site name" is easy to understand.
- When the screen changes in an SPA, change `document.title` too.
- For pagination of a list, use a form that shows it is a continuation, like "Search results (page 2)".
- The top page may be the site name alone.

## When checking

- **Settled**: violations of axe `document-title` (missing or empty) are non-conformant. A pass is not counted as conformant.
- **Remains**: compare the title with the headings and decide whether it describes the topic. With multiple pages, compare the groups with the same title ([cross-page](../cross-page.md)).
- **Do not assert**: do not mark non-conformant solely because the same title continues. Do not mark the absence of the site name non-conformant.
