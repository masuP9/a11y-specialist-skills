# 1.2.3 Audio Description or Media Alternative (Prerecorded)

- Scope: Prerecorded video in synchronized media
- Materials: Video and main audio, audio description or text alternative, interactive branches/outcomes, and access to alternatives
- Automated check: Tracks and links are candidates. Compare content, playback, and interaction to decide
- Sources checked: 2026-11-02. WCAG: Recommendation of 2024-12-12; Understanding / Techniques: living published pages. The following are summaries, not quotations.

## Evidence

### 1.2.3-E01 — Check the two Level A routes and applicability

- **Type / source:** [Normative — Audio Description or Media Alternative (Prerecorded)](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#audio-description-or-media-alternative-prerecorded), [Understanding — Intent](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/audio-description-or-media-alternative-prerecorded.html#intent).
- **Summary:** Check either audio description or an alternative for time-based media. Media clearly labeled as an alternative to text is excepted.
- **Conditions / limits:** Meeting this criterion with equivalent text does not automatically meet AA criterion 1.2.5. Distinguish video-only under 1.2.1. A summary or related material alone does not establish the exception.

### 1.2.3-E02 — Include video, audio, and interactive outcomes in text alternatives

- **Type / source:** [Technique — G69 “Description / Tests”](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/general/G69#description), [G58 “Description / Tests”](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/general/G58#description) (Sufficient together).
- **Summary:** Convey the final audio and visual information in sequence, with ways to achieve the same choices/outcomes for time-based interaction. G58 checks the link to the corresponding alternative and a way back.
- **Conditions / limits:** Dialogue-only transcripts are insufficient if visual information is missing. Compare production scripts with the final version. Do not make G58's adjacent link the only route; check that users can access the corresponding alternative.

### 1.2.3-E03 — Check the main audio and audio-description route

- **Type / source:** [Understanding — note in Intent](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/audio-description-or-media-alternative-prerecorded.html#intent), [Technique — G78 “Description / Tests”](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/general/G78#description) (Sufficient).
- **Summary:** Additional description is unnecessary when all important visual information is already in the main audio. Needed description may be in a selectable or default soundtrack.
- **Conditions / limits:** Audio presence alone is insufficient. Compare visual references such as “this,” silent demonstrations, and on-screen text with the video. For the boundary between standard and extended description, follow [1.2.5-E04 and its judgment lines](./1.2.5.md). For a single speaker without important time-based visual information, apply G203's static-alternative conditions in [1.2.5-E03](./1.2.5.md) (also Sufficient for 1.2.3).

## Judgment lines

- Pass when either audio description or equivalent text covering video, audio, and interaction works. Absence of one route alone is not Fail. Fail when both routes are confirmed inadequate. [Evidence: 1.2.3-E01–E03]
- Pass without an additional track when the main audio conveys all important visual information. Do not confuse this with merely having uninterrupted speech. [Evidence: 1.2.3-E03]
- Do not flag clearly labeled alternatives to text that add no information. A dialogue-only transcript added afterward does not establish this exception. [Evidence: 1.2.3-E01]
- A `descriptions` track, described-version link, or `aria-describedby` is a clue to the delivery method. Check correspondence, content, and usability instead of passing on presence alone. See [1.2.5-E05](./1.2.5.md) for track limitations. [Evidence: 1.2.3-E02–E03. Inspection procedure is ours]

## Judgment examples

- **Pass:** Usable text describes dialogue, sounds, screen operations, and displayed outcomes in sequence. Assess 1.2.5 separately. [Evidence: 1.2.3-E01–E02]
- **Pass:** The main audio describes all important actions and outcomes in a demonstration. Do not require an additional description track. [Evidence: 1.2.3-E03]
- **Exception; do not flag:** A video clearly identified as an alternative to explanatory text presents only its information. [Evidence: 1.2.3-E01]
- **Fail:** A training video relies on silent demonstrations but offers only a dialogue transcript and no audio description. [Evidence: 1.2.3-E01–E03]
- **Fail:** Interactive video offers purchases/choices, but its text alternative has no route to the same outcomes, and the audio-description route is also inadequate. [Evidence: 1.2.3-E02]
- **Needs review:** A described version is linked but cannot be played. Obtain the corresponding version and check its descriptions and usability. [Our interpretation: inspection example. Evidence: 1.2.3-E03]

## When building

- Choose a route for the target level. Level A text must include non-dialogue information and interaction; design the AA audio route with [1.2.5](./1.2.5.md). [Evidence: 1.2.3-E01–E03]
- Keep alternatives aligned with the final media, identify their correspondence, and provide access. [Evidence: 1.2.3-E02]

## When checking

- **Confirmed:** Results after comparing the chosen route's content, sequence, interaction, usability, and exceptions. [Evidence: 1.2.3-E01–E03]
- **Remaining:** Visual-only information, non-dialogue audio, branches/interaction, unplayable versions.
- **Do not overclaim:** Captions, transcripts, or tracks alone do not establish conformance. Record missing information/interaction and timestamps. Do not extend an A pass to AA as a whole. [Evidence: 1.2.3-E01–E03. Evidence format is our policy]
