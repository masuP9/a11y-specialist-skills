# 1.4.2 Audio Control

- Scope: whole page (autoplay of `<audio>` / `<video>`)
- Inputs needed: measurement (`audio` from auto-play-detection in `@a11y-skills/audit`)
- Automated checks: auto-play-detection, axe `no-autoplay-audio`

## Rationale

- [Understanding 1.4.2](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/audio-control.html) ([Japanese translation](https://waic.jp/translations/WCAG22/Understanding/audio-control.html))
  - Audio that plays automatically for more than 3 seconds needs a means to pause or stop it, or a means to control its volume independently of the overall system volume.
  - The means may be "near the beginning of the page"; it need not be the media's own controls.
  - 3 seconds or less is out of scope. Audio that starts playing by the user's action is also out of scope.
- [G60: within 3 seconds](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/general/G60), [G170: a means to stop at the beginning of the page](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/general/G170), [F23: plays for more than 3 seconds with no means to stop](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/failures/F23)

## Judgment lines

- The verdict is attached per page. `<audio>` is not an interactive component. [Our interpretation]
- From measurement (about 6 seconds after load, the length of time it was playing, unmuted, and at a volume above 0), candidates (needs review) are: content that played for more than 3 seconds without visible `controls`, content where autoplay did not start and could not be measured, and iframes whose content could not be read. [Our interpretation]
- One or more candidates is needs review; no `<audio>` / `<video>` is not applicable; present with no candidates is conformant. [Our interpretation]
- Non-conformant is not marked. Whether there is a means near the beginning of the page to stop or change the volume has not been confirmed by measurement. [Understanding] (the means need not be the media's own controls)
- Violations of axe `no-autoplay-audio` also stay as needs review. It only looks at the `controls` attribute and does not look at a means elsewhere on the page. [Our interpretation]
- The sound of an autoplaying `<video>` is treated the same way. The motion of the video is 2.2.2. [Understanding]

## When building

- Do not autoplay audio or video. If you do, start `muted`.
- If autoplay is needed, place a stop button or volume control near the beginning of the page (before the first interactive component).
- Do not add background music. If you do, keep it stopped by default and let the user start it.

## When checking

- **Settled**: none (neither non-conformant nor conformant is settled).
- **Remains**: if there are candidates, confirm visually whether there is a means near the beginning of the page to stop or change the volume.
- **Do not assert**: do not mark non-conformant solely because there are no `controls`. Do not mark conformant or not applicable while there are candidates.
