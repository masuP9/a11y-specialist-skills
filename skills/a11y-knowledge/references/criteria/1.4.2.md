# 1.4.2 Audio Control

- Scope: Automatically playing audio across the page, including sounds outside audio/video elements
- Needed: Actual audible duration and start conditions, plus operation of stopping or independent volume controls
- Automated checks: Audio candidates from auto-play-detection and axe no-autoplay-audio

- Sources checked: 2026-11-01. WCAG uses the 2024-12-12 Recommendation; Understanding / Techniques use the living WCAG 2.2 pages. The following are summaries, not quotations.

## Evidence

### 1.4.2-E01 — Check audio over three seconds and controls independent of system volume

- **Type / source:** [Normative — WCAG 2.2 “Audio Control”](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#audio-control), [Understanding — “Intent”](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/audio-control.html#intent), [Technique — G60 “Tests”](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/general/G60.html#tests) (Sufficient).
- **Summary:** Automatically playing sound lasting over three seconds needs stop/pause or volume adjustment down to zero independent of system volume. Muting the whole system is not a substitute.
- **Conditions / limits:** Distinguish sound that automatically stops within three seconds from a short recording that keeps looping. Assess visual movement separately under 2.2.2.

### 1.4.2-E02 — Playback after an arbitrary interaction is not necessarily intentional

- **Type / source:** [Understanding — explanation of plays automatically in “Intent”](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/audio-control.html#intent), [Technique — G171 “Examples”](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/general/G171.html#examples) (Sufficient).
- **Summary:** Check for intentional initiation through a label or context that makes the sound predictable. An unrelated click that triggers sound does not remove it from scope.
- **Conditions / limits:** A participant speaking in a two-way audio call is not treated as automatic playback. Distinguish independent call-volume controls as a best practice.

### 1.4.2-E03 — Check operation and reachability of controls outside the media element

- **Type / source:** [Technique — G170 “Description” and “Tests”](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/general/G170.html#tests) (Sufficient).
- **Summary:** A stop control near the beginning of the page can work. G170 checks early reading and tab order, a clear name, and keyboard operation.
- **Conditions / limits:** The controls attribute is not the only method. A button near the beginning alone is insufficient; check that it actually stops the relevant sound. Do not turn this Technique’s placement into the only normative requirement.

## Judgment lines

- Retain page-level reporting and the policy against automatic non-conformance from detection alone, because external controls remain unverified. When actual in-scope sound and missing controls are confirmed, assess against the evidence conditions. [Our interpretation: Reporting and inspection policy. Supporting evidence: 1.4.2-E01–E03]
- Audio candidates or unmeasured iframes require review. Retain scoped not-applicable when no audio/video or unmeasured iframes are detected, and scoped conformant when media exist without candidates. Do not extend conformance to Web Audio API, playback after observation, or browser-blocked playback. [Our interpretation: Tool mapping. Implementation: [normalizeAutoPlayDetection](../../../../packages/a11y-audit/src/utils/axe-format.ts)]
- The tool records about six seconds of media state and indications that sound will continue afterward. Silent tracks, actual usability of controls, and custom controls still need checking. [Our interpretation: Inspection scope. Implementation: [runAutoPlayDetection.ts](../../../../packages/a11y-audit/src/playwright/runAutoPlayDetection.ts)]
- Treat axe no-autoplay-audio violations as needing review. The inspected axe-core 4.14.0 checks controls, playback duration, loop, and related conditions, but does not verify other page controls. [Our interpretation: Tool mapping. Supporting evidence: 1.4.2-E03. Implementation inspected: `noAutoplayAudioEvaluate` in `node_modules/axe-core/axe.js`]

## When building

- Prefer intentional user initiation for audio/video. Start muted when automatically playing. [Our interpretation: Author implementation policy. Supporting evidence: 1.4.2-E01, 1.4.2-E02]
- Put automatic-audio controls near the beginning, early in interaction order. Retain the policy of background music being off by default and initiated by the user. [Our interpretation: Author recommendation. Supporting evidence: 1.4.2-E03]

## When checking

- **Confirmed:** No non-conformance from automatic candidates alone. Verify actual sound start/duration and independent control operation. [1.4.2-E01–E03]
- **Remaining:** Actual audible sound, volume down to zero, custom-button reachability, Web Audio API, and unmeasured iframes.
- **Do not assert:** Missing controls alone does not establish non-conformance. Do not mark conformant or not applicable when candidates exist. [Our interpretation: Inspection policy]
