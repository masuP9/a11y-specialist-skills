# 2.4.6 Headings and Labels

- Scope: Content presented as headings or labels, not only correctly tagged content
- Materials: Sections identified by headings, functions/input purposes identified by labels, image labels and context
- Automated check: DOM, screen, and page-snapshot provide comparison material. Name presence does not establish descriptiveness
- Sources checked: 2026-11-02. WCAG: Recommendation of 2024-12-12; Understanding / Techniques: living published pages. The following are summaries, not quotations.

## Evidence

### 2.4.6-E01 — Separate presence and markup from descriptiveness

- **Type / source:** [Normative — Headings and Labels](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#headings-and-labels), [Understanding — Intent](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/headings-and-labels.html#intent).
- **Summary:** Check whether provided headings/labels accurately describe topic or purpose. Do not require new headings or labels solely under this criterion.
- **Conditions / limits:** Separate associations/heading structure under 1.3.1, input labels/instructions under 3.3.2, and accessible names under 4.1.2. An appropriate name does not repair an incorrect visible label.

### 2.4.6-E02 — Compare headings with actual sections

- **Type / source:** [Technique — G130 “Description / Tests”](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/general/G130#description) (Sufficient), [Understanding — Examples](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/headings-and-labels.html#examples).
- **Summary:** Check that headings identify their sections in relation to the page and other sections.
- **Conditions / limits:** Do not automatically fail short headings or repeated “Summary”/“Conclusion” headings in different papers. Generic wording alone, without examining content, does not establish inadequacy.

### 2.4.6-E03 — Check clarity of purpose rather than label length

- **Type / source:** [Technique — G131 “Description / Tests”](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/general/G131#description) (Sufficient for 2.4.6), [Understanding — Intent / Examples](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/headings-and-labels.html#intent).
- **Summary:** Compare each component's purpose with its label. A word, character, or image commonly understood in context may suffice.
- **Conditions / limits:** A magnifying glass can mean search beside a search field or zoom beside an image. Neither image use nor lack of accompanying text alone establishes inadequacy. Check alternatives and associations separately.

## Judgment lines

- Pass when provided headings/labels describe actual topics, functions, or input purposes in context. Fail confirmed misleading descriptions or purposes that cannot be identified. [Evidence: 2.4.6-E01–E03]
- Passing name checks, heading tags, or label associations does not replace this SC's assessment. Conversely, a missing heading tag on meaningful visible heading content alone is not a 2.4.6 failure. [Evidence: 2.4.6-E01]
- Do not reassign missing names to 2.4.6. Preserve [4.1.2's author policy](./4.1.2.md) for dialog/alertdialog names; assess this criterion when there is independent evidence such as a misleading visible heading. [Our interpretation: existing audit division. Evidence: 4.1.2-E05, 2.4.6-E01]

## Judgment examples

- **Pass:** Individual papers use “Summary” and “Conclusion” matching their content. Repetition across papers alone is not an issue. [Evidence: 2.4.6-E02]
- **Pass:** A magnifying glass beside a search field denotes search, with “Search” as its alternative. Do not universally demand an added text label. [Evidence: 2.4.6-E03]
- **Do not flag under this SC:** A visible “Shipping address” heading accurately describes its section but lacks heading markup. Assess structure under 1.3.1. [Evidence: 2.4.6-E01]
- **Fail:** A field requesting a telephone number is visibly labeled “Email address”. An accessible name of “Telephone number” does not repair the incorrect visible label. [Our interpretation: comparison example. Evidence: 2.4.6-E01, E03]
- **Needs review:** Only a “Settings” heading is available in the tree, without its section. Obtain corresponding content and page context. [Our interpretation: inspection example. Evidence: 2.4.6-E02]

## When building

- Match headings to section content and labels to actual purposes. More words are not automatically better; check whether short expressions or images are understood in context. [Evidence: 2.4.6-E02–E03]

## When checking

- **Implementation checked:** axe-core 4.14.0 has no rule tagged wcag246. `page-snapshot` provides names/headings as material and leaves verdict buckets empty. Passes from `label` / `button-name` do not establish descriptiveness.
- **Confirmed:** Results of comparing headings with content and labels with component purposes. Include image labels and visible headings absent from the tree's heading structure. [Evidence: 2.4.6-E01–E03]
- **Remaining:** If content/function is unavailable, identify the missing material for review. Do not fail based only on brevity, duplication, or tags.
