# 3.1.1 Language of Page

- Scope: whole page; implementation/tool checks here focus on HTML
- Inputs needed: html lang, xml:lang and document format as needed, representative content and language distribution
- Automated checks: axe `html-has-lang`, `html-lang-valid`, and `html-xml-lang-mismatch`
- Sources checked: 2026-11-02. WCAG is the 2024-12-12 Recommendation; Understanding / Techniques are living public pages. The following are summaries, not quotations.

## Rationale

### 3.1.1-E01 — Compare the default language with the content

- **Type / source:** [Normative — Language of Page](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#language-of-page), [Understanding — Intent](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/language-of-page.html#intent).
- **Summary:** Use the predominant language on multilingual pages; when languages are used equally, choose the first. Do not decide from the beginning of navigation alone.
- **Conditions / limits:** Evaluate passages in another language under 3.1.2. Obtain representative content when sparse or unavailable content prevents judgment.

### 3.1.1-E02 — Separate attribute presence, language tags, and content correspondence

- **Type / source:** [Technique — H57, Tests](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/html/H57#tests) (Sufficient for HTML).
- **Summary:** Check that html has lang, its value follows BCP 47, and it reflects the page's primary language. Presence alone is insufficient.
- **Conditions / limits:** Do not apply H57 directly to PDF or other formats. Distinguish primary-language matching from full-tag syntax validation. The policy below of not scoring region/script differences is the author's.

## Judgment lines

- Missing/empty default-language identification in HTML fails. A valid primary language clearly different from the content also fails. When content is unavailable, correspondence remains unverified even with an attribute. [Evidence: 3.1.1-E01–E02]
- Retain the policy of comparing primary languages without failing solely for region/script differences. This does not exempt malformed tags. [Our interpretation: author policy setting assessment granularity at primary language. Supporting evidence: 3.1.1-E02]
- For equally used languages, choose the first. Inspect the main content and distribution without requiring exhaustive mechanical character counts. [Evidence: 3.1.1-E01; counting method is author inspection policy]
- axe-core 4.14.0 `html-lang-valid` checks the primary-language part, not all of BCP 47. A pass does not establish valid later subtags or correspondence to content. `html-xml-lang-mismatch` also compares primary languages; check document format and both attributes. [Our interpretation: tool mapping. Implementation checked: `node_modules/axe-core/axe.js`, `getBaseLang`, `validLangEvaluate`, `hasLangEvaluate`]

## Judgment examples

- **Conformant:** Predominantly Japanese content with `lang="ja"`. English product names alone do not fail 3.1.1. [Evidence: 3.1.1-E01–E02]
- **Non-conformant:** Switching an English page to Japanese leaves `lang="en"` while the main content is Japanese. [Evidence: 3.1.1-E01–E02]
- **Do not flag under author policy:** English content has valid `en-US` though its regional usage resembles British English. Do not fail solely for the region difference. [Our interpretation: primary-language matching example. Supporting evidence: 3.1.1-E02]
- **Conformant:** German and English are used about equally, German comes first, and the default is `de`. Do not overturn solely for a minor character-count difference. [Evidence: 3.1.1-E01; character-count treatment is an author application example]
- **Needs review:** Lang was collected, but content requires login and only navigation in another language was obtained. Obtain the main content. [Our interpretation: inspection policy. Evidence: 3.1.1-E01]

## When building

- Set the page's primary language in the HTML template and update it with content on language changes. Do not hard-code Japanese for every language. [Evidence: 3.1.1-E01–E02]
- Check language changes within content separately under 3.1.2. [Evidence: 3.1.1-E01]

## When checking

- **Settled:** Missing/empty language identification in HTML, confirmed invalid identification, or clear mismatch with the main content. [3.1.1-E01–E02]
- **Remains:** Main-content comparison, equally used languages, subtags not checked by the tool, and unavailable documents.
- **Do not assert:** Full SC conformance from axe passes, or failure solely from region differences or passages in another language. [Evidence: 3.1.1-E01–E02; region differences follow the author policy above]
