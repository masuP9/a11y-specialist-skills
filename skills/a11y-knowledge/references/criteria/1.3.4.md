# 1.3.4 Orientation

- Scope: whole page
- Inputs needed: measurement (orientation-check from `@a11y-skills/audit`)
- Automated checks: orientation-check

## Rationale

- [Understanding 1.3.4](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/orientation.html) ([Japanese translation](https://waic.jp/translations/WCAG22/Understanding/orientation.html))
  - Content must be usable in both portrait and landscape. The only exception is when a specific orientation is essential (a piano app, reading a bank check).
  - The problem is the means of locking orientation (hiding or rotating the display with the CSS `orientation` media query, meta tags, JavaScript).
- [F97: locking orientation and making content unusable](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/failures/F97), [F100: a message prompting the user to change orientation](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/failures/F100)

## Judgment lines

- If orientation is not locked, conformant. Even if locked, it is an exception when the content can be said to make that orientation essential. [Understanding]
- Showing "please rotate your device" and making the content unusable also counts as locking. [Understanding] (F100)

## When building

- Do not hide or rotate content with `@media (orientation: ...)`.
- Locking orientation is needed only when the content depends on orientation (musical instruments, some games).

## When checking

- **Settled**: if orientation-check reports "orientation is locked", check whether it falls under the exception.
- **Remains**: whether the lock falls under the "essential" exception. Judge by looking at the content.
- **Do not assert**: do not mark conformant without measurement.
